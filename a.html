The problem

Six outlets. One campus. Nobody wants to walk alone.

Every hostel — H1, H2, Old Boys Hostel, Girls Hostel, Guest House — is a short walk from the places students actually want something from. But that walk adds up when it's the fifth trip of the day, and most of the time, someone from your own hostel is already headed there.

The solution

Two ways to open the app. One shared board.

"I want something"

Post a request — item, outlet, quantity, and where to deliver it. If someone's already headed there, it's matched instantly. If not, it waits for the next person going.

"I'm going"

Post a trip before you leave, and every open request for that exact outlet shows up, sorted so you only see the ones actually on your way back to your hostel.

No riders. No delivery fee. No new business — just the trips your hostel already makes, made visible to each other.

Trust & safety

Built for real cash, real people, real hostels

No wallet. No payment gateway. Money changes hands physically, the way it already does between hostel-mates — the app's job is just to make sure the right two people completed the exchange.

🔐

One-time code

Generated the moment an item is bought. Only shared after cash is handed over — pay and confirm happen together, not one before the other.

⭐

Trust score

Visible on every profile before you match with someone. Verified college identity plus a real history — most of the trust work happens before a trade starts.

🚩

Red flag

An unresolved handoff — code never entered, item never arrived — is marked and reviewable, so a pattern is visible without accusing anyone outright.
